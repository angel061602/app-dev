# My Favorite Series: Game of Thrones

**Game of Thrones** is an epic fantasy drama television series adapted from George R.R. Martin's *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, set in the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos.

## Why I Love It
* **Complex Characters:** Almost every character has deep motivations, and no one is purely good or purely evil.
* *Unpredictable Plot Twists:* Major events and betrayals keep every episode intense and surprising.
* **Massive World-Building:** The politics, rival houses, and lore of the Seven Kingdoms make the story feel rich and immersive.

> "When you play the game of thrones, you win or you die." — *Cersei Lannister*
